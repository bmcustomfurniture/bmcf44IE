About Us

BM Custom Furniture continues the tradition of made-to-measure craftsmanship across Leinster. With over 20 years of experience, we design, manufacture, and install custom furniture for bedrooms, bathrooms, living spaces, and home offices. Our prices are typically 30% to 50% lower than many competitors while maintaining a high standard of quality and finish, and we are trusted by hundreds of happy customers.

We create functional, elegant furniture tailored to your home and how you live. From survey and design through to installation, we provide a complete service.
